Chris Bowen, 31 August 2023. The cost is now **$12 billion**. First power 2027, all units **December 2028**. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2023/aug/31/snowy-hydro-2-project-scheme-billion-cost-blowout-kosciuszko-national-park): “put the important project **back on track**.” The $12 billion is “obviously very, very substantial.” Same constraints “all major infrastructure projects around the world are experiencing. That’s just a fact.”

This card files that track. It does not file “sack the minister.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [ANAO](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/performance-audit/delivery-of-snowy-two-point-zero): until 11 May 2023 Snowy told the shareholder departments the job was “**on track**” on cost and schedule. Separately, management was already reporting delivery pressures and the need for a reassessment.
- 3 October 2025, [Dennis Barnes](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-10-03/snowy-hydro-cost-blowout-assessment-deliver-project/105775866): “we’re **not going to achieve the cost target** we’ve set.” He took the miss. Timeline? “Pretty **comfortable**.” 67 per cent complete, still December 2028.
- [ANAO, June 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-18/auditors-say-snowy-2-0-not-holding-contractors-accountable/106700336): management since the reset has been “**partly effective**.” “**Significant deficiencies**.” **No agreed baseline schedule.** No robust forecast of cost to complete. **$11.1 billion** spent at 73 per cent. October 2025 note to the auditor: expenditure “**expected to exceed**” the approved **$12 billion**; more Commonwealth support “expected” by the end of FY26. Bowen’s office, same week as the audit: “sensible recommendations.” Still: deliver it.

Grammar: back on track, $12 billion, December 2028. Logic: a reset is not a rail. Rhetoric: that’s just a fact.
