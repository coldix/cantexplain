28/10/2026. Labor cites an independent think tank.

**What the Australia Institute paper says** (September 2026):

- The Liberals' $22 billion hiring freeze would need about 31,700 fewer positions by 2036-37.
- Measured against 58,169 public servants, not counting schools or hospitals.
- Verdict on the Liberal plan: implausible.

The hospital numbers and the $40 billion are not in it.
