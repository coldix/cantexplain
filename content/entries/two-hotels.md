Hotel quarantine was the wall: returned travellers in, virus not out. [18 August 2020](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2020-08-18/victoria-covid-crisis-traced-back-to-seven-travellers-inquiry/12568408), the Coate inquiry heard the genome. Dr Charles Alpren: “approximately **99 per cent** of current cases… have arisen from **Rydges or Stamford**.” Two hotels. Seven travellers at the start of the chains.

This card files that wall. It does not file “let everyone walk in.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [Andrews, December](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2020/dec/21/daniel-andrews-apologises-for-botched-hotel-quarantine-program-in-victoria): apologised. Blamed lack of time. National cabinet ordered 14 days on 27 March. Victoria stood it up in **36 hours**. Coate: nobody owned the private-security decision. The report linked those outbreaks to the second wave — and, in the coverage of the final report, **801 deaths**.
- February 2021, **Holiday Inn** at the airport leaked again. Then the [five-day circuit-breaker](https://cantexplain.au/hall/circuit-breaker).
- A sealed hotel is a building. Aerosol in a corridor is another meter. The slogan was containment.

Grammar: 99 per cent, two hotels. Logic: 36 hours is not a wall. Rhetoric: quarantine.
