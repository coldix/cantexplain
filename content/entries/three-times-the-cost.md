Daniel Andrews, 18 July 2023. Victoria is out. [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-07-18/commonwealth-games-regional-victoria-scrapped-daniel-andrews/102613718): the cost “is **not the $2.6 billion** which was budgeted and allocated.” It is “**three times** the cost estimated and budgeted for last year.” At least **$6 billion**. In the days after, **$6–7 billion**.

This card files those two numbers. It does not file “they should have hosted at any price.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [VAGO, March 2024](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/withdrawal-2026-commonwealth-games/): the original budget was **unrealistically low**. DJSIR put a high-case of **$3.2 billion** in front of the minister. The submission that went to government used the **low-case $2.7 billion**. The 2022–23 Budget printed **$2.6 billion**. VAGO: “unwarranted optimism bias.” At worst, “unrealistic and misleading.”
- The $6.9 billion the government later released was **“overstated and not transparent.”** It stacked **$2 billion** of extra “cost pressures” on top of a budget that already held **$1 billion** of contingency for the same risks. Fairly presented: about **$5.9 billion**. The minister’s own July minute had said **$5–6 billion**.
- [ABC, same audit day](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-03-20/commonwealth-games-victoria-cancelled-cost-revealed-report/103608858). Premier Jacinta Allan, who had been minister for Games delivery: the report “**confirm[s] that we made the right decision**.” The auditor had just taken a billion off the slide.

Grammar: not $2.6, three times, $6–7. Logic: a best-case and a double-count are not a pair of facts. Rhetoric: what's become clear.
