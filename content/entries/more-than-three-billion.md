[Premier of Victoria, 12 April 2022](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/regional-victoria-hosting-2026-commonwealth-games). Victoria is the host. The first number on the page is not the cost. It is the gift: the Games “will contribute **more than $3 billion** to Victoria’s economy.” Jobs before, during, after. A lasting legacy of affordable housing and world-class sports facilities.

This card files that floor. It does not file “sport never pays.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [Victorian Auditor-General, March 2024](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/withdrawal-2026-commonwealth-games/): the business case **overstated the benefits** and underestimated the costs. Around **20 per cent** of the predicted benefit was avoided health costs from physical activity and civic pride. Treasury called those **speculative** and asked them taken out. They stayed in the ratio.
- Updated business case, March 2022: benefit–cost **0.7 to 1.6**. Strip the speculative benefits and the questionable cost cuts and VAGO’s range is **0.59 to 0.95**. Projects under 1.0 usually do not proceed.
- “More than $3 billion” is a one-way number. The case was a band. The low end did not get a press release.

Grammar: more than, will contribute. Logic: civic pride is not a receipt. Rhetoric: the gift first.
