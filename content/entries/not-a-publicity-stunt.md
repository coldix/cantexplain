Greta Thunberg, 28 September 2025, on the BBC, sailing with the Global Sumud Flotilla. Israel had called the June boat a “selfie yacht.” She said nobody would risk their life for a stunt. The purpose, she said, was to “break Israel’s illegal and inhumane siege on Gaza by sea.” Also: “not just a humanitarian mission” — a message that the people will step up.

This card files that verb. It does not file “Greta is a hypocrite” and it does not file “the blockade is fine.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- The first trip, [*Madleen*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/June_2025_Gaza_Freedom_Flotilla), left Catania on 1 June 2025. The [Freedom Flotilla Coalition](https://freedomflotilla.org/2025/06/01/a-month-after-conscience-attack-freedom-flotilla-ship-madleen-sets-sail-for-gaza/) called the cargo “urgently needed supplies” and the job “break the siege.” The hold: **100 kg of flour, 250 kg of rice**, baby formula, diapers, medical kits, crutches. Twelve people. [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cx25grpxzd3o): a **token** amount. [AP](https://apnews.com/article/mideast-wars-gaza-flotilla-greta-thunberg-b64e897fb37ee374e4494381304ff9be): Israeli officials said **less than a truckload**.
- Israeli navy boarded *Madleen* about 185 km from Gaza on 9 June. Thunberg was deported the next day. She called it a kidnapping. The rice did not open a corridor.
- She went again in September. Same slogan. [Intercepted again](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0lk292jww4o), 1–2 October 2025, with hundreds of others. Same ending.

A siege is a logistics problem. 350 kilos of grain is a press list. Open both holds.
