25 March 2020. A government SMS, 150 characters, endorsed by Chief Medical Officer Brendan Murphy. [Greg Hunt’s release](https://www.health.gov.au/ministers/the-hon-greg-hunt-mp/media/australian-government-text-message-regarding-coronavirus-prevention-steps): “To stop the spread, stay **1.5m** from others.” Also: “Be at least 1.5 metres away from **everyone**.”

This card files that verb. It does not file “stand where you like.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- 1.5 m is a **droplet** heuristic. Large drops fall. Fine aerosols hang around indoors and do not stop at a line of tape.
- “Stop the spread” is a hard stop. Distance can cut **close-range** spray. It is not a switch for an airborne virus in a badly ventilated room.
- The same release stacked wash hands, stay home if sick, don’t touch your face. Those are other meters. The number that stuck was one-point-five.

Grammar: 1.5 m, droplets. Logic: that is not “stop.” Rhetoric: a text message to every phone.
