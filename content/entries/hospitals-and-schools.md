Daniel Andrews, 18 July 2023. The moral of the cancellation. [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-07-18/commonwealth-games-regional-victoria-scrapped-daniel-andrews/102613718): “I will **not take money out of hospitals and schools**” for a Games at three times the budget. The [Premier's release](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/more-homes-sports-upgrades-gippsland) the same day: “**more than $6 billion** for a **12-day sporting event** just doesn’t make sense.” Not at any price.

This card files that ledger. It does not file “always host.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [VAGO](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/withdrawal-2026-commonwealth-games/): bidding, planning and walking away cost Victorians **over $589 million** with “**no discernible benefit**.” **$380 million** of that was the settlement with the Games bodies. The rest was staff, the organising company, drawings for venues that did not open. “This waste would have been avoided” if the advice before the bid had been frank.
- [19 August 2023](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-08-19/victoria-commonwealth-games-compensation-settlement/102750854), Andrews on the $380 million: “the **best outcome** that Victoria could get.” “Not a dollar more.”
- July 2026, Glasgow opened a slimmed Games on existing venues. [AFR](https://www.afr.com/companies/sport/welcome-to-glasgow-s-cut-price-commonwealth-games-thanks-victoria-20260723-p60hqa): “the finest **cut-price** Commonwealth Games that **Victorian taxpayers’ money** can buy.” [SportsPro](https://www.sportspro.com/features/decision-makers/politics-and-governance/commonwealth-games-2026-glasgow-katie-sadleir-hosting-model/), quoting Commonwealth Sport: about **£160 million** to run; **most** of it from a **£100 million** slice of Victoria’s settlement. Hospitals and schools did not receive that transfer. Glasgow did.

Grammar: will not take, hospitals, schools. Logic: a cancellation cheque is still an outlay. Rhetoric: 12-day event.
