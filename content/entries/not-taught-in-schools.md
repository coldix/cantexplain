Randi Weingarten, president of the American Federation of Teachers, [TEACH 2021](https://www.aft.org/press/speeches/safe-and-welcoming-school-year-all), 6 July 2021: “Let’s be clear: **critical race theory is not taught** in elementary schools or high schools.” It is, she said, a law-school method. The same speech vowed to defend teachers against “culture warriors” who “label any discussion of race” as CRT. [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/critical-race-theory-teachers-union-honest-history/) ran the line as the union’s position.

This card files that sentence. It does not file “no child should hear that slavery happened.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- The same AFT conference put [Ibram X. Kendi](https://www.penguin.co.uk/discover/articles/ibram-x-kendi-definition-of-antiracist) on the program. His classroom book says the only remedy to racist discrimination is **antiracist discrimination**.
- The Pulitzer Center’s [1619 Project curriculum](https://pulitzercenter.org/education/k-12-programs-and-resources/1619-project-education-portal) was already in **thousands of K-12 classrooms**. Medill, July 2020: more than **4,500 schools**.
- Weingarten’s own definition of CRT — a method that “helps analyze whether **systemic racism** exists” — is the same noun the Australian *Racism. No Way!* site and ACARA’s invasion line already put in front of children. The brand stays in law school. The nouns go to Year 4.

Grammar: is not taught. Logic: a brand can be absent while the method is assigned. Rhetoric: honest history.
