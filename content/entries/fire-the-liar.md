One Nation, June 2026. The fundraising page is titled **FIRE THE LIAR!** Under that: “Albo Lied about,” then ten bullets, then “How many more lies will Albo tell before the end of his term in Government?” No source, date, or quote under any item. [Michelle Grattan](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-12/anthony-albanese-one-nation-tax-budget-pauline-hanson-grattan/106775114) recorded the slogan the week it launched. The *Tele* ran the ad: “Stop Labor. Fire the Liar. Donate Now.”

This card files that list. It does not file “Albanese is a liar” and it does not file “Albanese kept his word.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- **$275.** [3 December 2021](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-05-19/promise-check-cut-power-bills-by-275-dollars/101791146): Albanese said bills would fall “from the current level by $275 for household[s] by 2025.” Asked if that was possible: “I don’t think, I know. I know because we have done the modelling.” He was still saying it on 18 May 2022. [ABS, year to June 2026](https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-38-year-june-2026): household electricity **+22.4%**, “largely because government rebates… have ended.” That is not a $275 cut by 2025.
- **Stage 3.** After the 2022 election he said “my word is my bond” on the legislated cuts. In January 2024 the government [retooled them](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2024-02-08/stage-3-tax-cuts-my-word-is-my-bond/103443980). A broken word. Also the template for the next one.
- **Negative gearing and CGT.** Ruled out on the 2025 hustings. [Budget night, 12 May 2026](https://budget.gov.au/content/04-tax-reform.htm): negative gearing limited to new builds from 1 July 2027; the 50% CGT discount replaced with inflation indexation and a 30% minimum tax. Now [law](https://www.ato.gov.au/about-ato/new-legislation/in-detail/individuals/tax-reform-boosting-home-ownership-reforming-negative-gearing-and-capital-gains-tax). On 5 June, asked why he ruled it out then did it: “We have changed that, we have changed our position… just as we changed our position in the last term on the stage three tax cuts.” Grattan: breaking your word is not, by itself, proof you *lied* (intent to deceive). It is still a breach.

**What the list also filed as a lie:**

- **Voice to Parliament.** Labor promised a referendum and [held it](https://www.rmit.edu.au/about/schools-colleges/media-and-communication/industry/promise-tracker/voice-to-parliament-referendum) (14 October 2023). It lost. A failed vote is not a secret second position.
- **Falling off a stage.** A stumble. It does not have a Hansard.
- **Immigration Levels, Medicare and Bulk Billing, Isis Brides.** On the page. No cited sentence.

Three of the ten bullets have a dated promise and a later act. One is a referendum they held. One is gravity. The heading is the same.

The nickname arrived fully formed. The charge sheet mixed the ledger with the trip.
