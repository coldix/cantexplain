[Racism. No Way!](https://racismnoway.com.au/about-racism/understanding-racism/white-privilege/) is the national anti-racism site for Australian schools, “managed by the NSW Department of Education on behalf of all Australian schools.” Its definition: **white privilege** is “the implicit societal advantages afforded to White people.” Francis Kendall is quoted: an “institutional… set of benefits granted to those of us who, by race, resemble the people who dominate.” Students are told to see society “**systemically and structurally**.” [Privilege for sale](https://racismnoway.com.au/about-racism/understanding-racism/privilege-for-sale-activity/) is a Years 9–10 activity. The footnote is Peggy McIntosh, 1989, Massachusetts.

This card files that definition. It does not file “racism never happens in a classroom.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- The same site’s [institutional racism](https://racismnoway.com.au/about-racism/understanding-racism/institutional-racism/) page: racism “structured into political and social institutions,” including when governments “do not view themselves as racist.” That is the CRT noun, without the brand.
- The privilege activity cites McIntosh’s *Invisible Knapsack* and a US “Privilege For Sale” kit. The note on the page: race is “a social construct.” The lesson still sorts children by how much privilege they can buy.
- The [IPA Schools Program](https://schoolsprogram.org.au/our-vision) called the HASS years “undertones of Critical Race Theory.” The official URL does not say CRT. It says white privilege, systemic, institutional.

Grammar: afforded to White people. Logic: a US essay is not an Australian census. Rhetoric: privilege.
