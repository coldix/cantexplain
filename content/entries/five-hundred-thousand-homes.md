Malcolm Turnbull, 21 December 2017, the night the feasibility study dropped. [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-12-21/snowy-hydro-2.0-could-actually-cost-as-much-as-$4.5b/9277368): a “**giant 2,000 megawatt battery**,” “enough power for **500,000 homes**.” Same week in March he had already tried the other unit: in one hour, **20 times** South Australia’s proposed 100 MW battery, “constantly for **almost a week**,” “**350,000 MW hours** over seven days.”

This card files that mix. It does not file “the turbines are fake.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- **2,000 MW** is power — how hard the tap can run. **350 GWh** is energy — how long the tank lasts at that rate. **500,000 homes** is a power slogan. **A week** is an energy slogan. They are not the same house-count.
- The 2023 reset quietly lifted the tap to **2,200 MW** and swapped the house-count for [three million homes for a week](https://cantexplain.au/hall/three-million-homes). The tank was always ~350 GWh. The homes multiplied.
- Paul Broad, March 2017: it would “**absolutely**” help keep the lights on. The lights it is keeping, in 2026, are still the old scheme’s. First power is listed for late 2027.

Grammar: giant battery, 2,000 MW, 500,000 homes. Logic: a tap is not a tank. Rhetoric: 20 times.
