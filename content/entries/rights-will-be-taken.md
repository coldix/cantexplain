Kaylee, Year 8, Melbourne, 13 August 2026. Mum asked for reasoning and got a four-page essay. The essay is not in the paper. Asked why Hanson should not be in charge, Kaylee said: “I just don’t think she should be able to make those kinds of choices.”

Same day, same city, Georgia, 15, told the [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-13/one-nation-students-protest-pauline-hanson/107033644) she was tired of “the injustices that Pauline Hanson is threatening … if they go into office.” No office, bill, or date attached.

**What is on the record, and is not this claim:**

- Hanson did say “monocultural” Australia at the Press Club in June. That is a real sentence. It is not a drafted law that strips race or queer rights from Year 8s.
- One Nation’s rise is real: [Roy Morgan 26% primary](https://www.roymorgan.com/findings/10307-federal-voting-intention-august-10-2026) the week of the walkout.
- NUS had a sharper, checkable complaint: her Senate votes against free TAFE, school funding and HECS relief. That belongs in a voting-record argument. It was not what Kaylee was asked, and not what she answered.

Feeling that rights are about to go is a feeling. The hall files the gap between that feeling and the missing instrument.
