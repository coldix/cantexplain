[ABC Health, 25 March 2020](https://www.abc.net.au/news/health/2020-03-25/coronavirus-covid-19-face-mask-surgical-mask-protection/12088314): community spreading. Do you need a mask? “If you're well… the answer is **probably no**.” Headline: not recommended for healthy individuals.

[23 July 2020](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2020-07-23/face-masks-compulsory-in-melbourne-as-covid-19-fight-intensifies/12484876), same city: a face covering in public or a **$200 fine**. Surgical, **cloth**, or **a scarf**. Daniel Andrews: deeply grateful to Victorians wearing masks and “making sacrifices” to **stop the spread**.

This card files that U-turn. It does not file “masks never worked.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- Four months. Same virus. Official Australia went from “probably no” to a scarf as a legal instrument.
- [Healthdirect](https://www.healthdirect.gov.au/face-masks) still says cloth gives “the **least** protection” from droplets and aerosols — “but they can still help stop the spread.” Least, and stop: two verbs on one page.
- A fitted respirator and a tea towel are not the same meter. The slogan treated them as one covering.

Grammar: well / not well, March. Logic: a scarf is not a P2. Rhetoric: stop the spread, or $200.
