18 July 2023, the same morning the Games died. [Premier's release](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/more-homes-sports-upgrades-gippsland): a **$2 billion** package so regional Victoria “**still receives all the benefits**” the Games would have brought. “**Every one** of the permanent new and upgraded sporting infrastructure projects… **will still proceed** — and all will be **completed as planned**.” Jacinta Allan, then minister for Games delivery: every one of those facilities “**will be delivered**.” Harriet Shing: “We’ll deliver **all** the housing, sports infrastructure and tourism legacy benefits.”

On [ABC Radio](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-07-18/victoria-commonwealth-games-announcement-daniel-andrews/102613156) that afternoon, Allan again: the point of the bid was lasting housing and infrastructure. “**We are still going to do that.**”

This card files that “all.” It does not file “build nothing in the regions.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- $2 billion is **$600 million** less than the $2.6 billion Games line it replaced. A third of the consolation is a **$1 billion Regional Housing Fund** for “more than **1,300**” new homes.
- Victorian Parliament’s select committee, [final report, March 2025](https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/495902/contentassets/d674233824484c9685aa8acbdb1d0a37/final-report-for-website-lc-comm-games-bid-60-03_final-report.pdf), Finding 21: the $2 billion package “**has not yet been fully delivered**.” The committee’s own words: it “largely remains in the **planning or advanced planning** stages.”
- [Homes Victoria](https://www.homes.vic.gov.au/regional-housing-fund), still on the page: more than **630** homes “completed or under construction,” of which **377** are “new and upgraded” already finished — and **187** of those 377 are **existing homes upgraded**. Fund completion is listed as **2028**. “All the benefits,” “completed as planned,” and a 2028 finish line are three clocks.

Grammar: all, every one, still, as planned. Logic: an upgrade is not a new house. Rhetoric: lasting legacy, without the Games.
