Greg Hunt, 10 December 2021, [joint release with the Prime Minister](https://pmtranscripts.pmc.gov.au/release/transcript-43700): ATAGI has ticked 5-to-11s. Parents who vaccinate “have done **everything possible** to keep their child **safe from this virus**.” Also: it will “help prevent children **passing the virus on**” to siblings, grandparents, the wider community.

This card files that guarantee. It does not file “skip the jab.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [TGA, January](https://cantexplain.au/hall/ninety-five-per-cent): the 95 was **symptomatic** disease, original strain. Transmission **unclear**. Asymptomatic **not tested**. Duration **unclear**.
- “Everything possible” and “safe from this virus” are a force field. A two-dose course was a risk reduction against severe disease. It was not a sealed room.
- The 5–11 rollout opened in January 2022, into **Omicron**. School-age infection among the vaccinated was not a rumour. It was the season.

Grammar: everything, safe, this virus. Logic: a trial endpoint is not a childhood immunity. Rhetoric: reassure all parents.
