Chris Bowen and Katy Gallagher, 31 August 2023, the $12 billion reset day. [Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2023/aug/31/snowy-hydro-2-project-scheme-billion-cost-blowout-kosciuszko-national-park): Snowy 2.0 will provide “enough renewable energy and storage to power **three million homes for a week**.” [Snowy Hydro’s own page](https://www.snowyhydro.com.au/snowy-20/about/) still says it: **2,200 MW**, three million homes, “about **26 million** home batteries,” “**more than half**” of AEMO’s 649 GWh storage need, “**350 GWh**.” Bowen, [June 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-18/auditors-say-snowy-2-0-not-holding-contractors-accountable/106700336), same houses: “**3 million homes per week**,” “downward pressure on prices.”

This card files that week. It does not file “storage is a con.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- 2017’s house-count was [**500,000**](https://cantexplain.au/hall/five-hundred-thousand-homes), on **2,000 MW**. The tank was already “almost a week” / **350 GWh**. The 2023 slogan kept the tank and multiplied the houses. Power and energy swapped seats.
- [ABC, June 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-03/billions-over-budget-years-late-how-snowy-hydro-2-got-here/106706518), former energy executive Ted Woodley: theoretically it can run a week. “In practical terms it **probably never will**.” The upper pond has to be refilled when prices are low enough. That can take “weeks or months.” That is one engineer. The slogan does not mention a refill.
- HumeLink — the line that gets the week to the homes — is a separate **$5 billion** on NSW bills. The three million are not yet attached.

Grammar: three million, a week, 26 million batteries. Logic: a full Tantangara is a condition, not a headline. Rhetoric: more than half.
