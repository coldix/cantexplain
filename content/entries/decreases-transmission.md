Greg Hunt, 2 September 2021, [press conference](https://www.health.gov.au/ministers/the-hon-greg-hunt-mp/media/press-conference-in-canberra-on-2-september-2021-about-the-vaccination-rollout-and-national-plan-for-covid-response): vaccinate more, the rate goes up, “it **decreases transmission** in the community. It protects children.”

A fortnight earlier, [Scott Morrison](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2021/aug/22/high-covid-case-numbers-should-not-delay-australias-reopening-pm-says) on the Doherty National Plan: above **70 and 80 per cent**, lockdowns do “**more harm than good**.” Asked if they end: “**highly unlikely** and targeted.”

This card files that switch. It does not file “vaccines never cut severe disease.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- [TGA, 25 January 2021](https://cantexplain.au/hall/ninety-five-per-cent): **unclear** if the vaccine reduces transmission. Same unknowns page as the 95 per cent.
- Doherty underwrote the plan. One of its architects, James McCaw, told the Guardian case numbers were too high to drop lockdowns even at 70%. The slogan was the percentage. The modeller was still counting cases.
- Omicron later ran through a double-dosed country. Transmission was not a switch that flipped at 80.

Grammar: decreases, community, 80%. Logic: unknown on approval day is not a National Plan lever. Rhetoric: highly unlikely.
