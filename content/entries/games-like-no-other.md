Daniel Andrews, Eureka Stadium, April 2022. Victoria had just been handed the 2026 Commonwealth Games. [ABC, quoting the day](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-07-18/commonwealth-games-regional-victoria-scrapped-daniel-andrews/102613718): “**A games like no other, exactly what we promised.**” Also: “We're **very, very confident** that this will deliver jobs, it'll deliver better facilities, better housing and it'll be a showcase to the world.”

The [Premier's release](https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/regional-victoria-hosting-2026-commonwealth-games) the same week: “we **can't wait to welcome the world** to all of our state.” Four regional hubs. Opening at the MCG. Motto on the bid: a Games like no other, in a place like no other.

This card files that promise. It does not file “never bid for a Games.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- Fifteen months later the same Premier [walked away](https://cantexplain.au/hall/three-times-the-cost). No world at Eureka. No villages as villages. The motto is what was left.
- Host cities usually get seven or eight years. Victoria signed for **four**. [VAGO](https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/withdrawal-2026-commonwealth-games/): a regional, multi-city model that had never been run, and the business case did not consult the councils that owned the venues.
- “Very, very confident” is a temperature. A contract is a date.

Grammar: like no other, promised, welcome the world. Logic: a motto is not a village. Rhetoric: showcase.
