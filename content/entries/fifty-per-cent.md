CSIRO, still on the [eReefs page](https://www.csiro.au/en/research/natural-environment/oceans/ereefs-management-system): coral cover “has declined by **50 per cent** in just **27 years**.” The footnote is De’ath, Fabricius, Sweatman and Puotinen, *PNAS* 2012 — AIMS authors, 1985–2012. That paper is the mint.

This card files that fraction. It does not file “no coral died.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- The same [PNAS paper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.1208909109) split the loss: tropical cyclones **48%**, crown-of-thorns **42%**, bleaching **10%**. The slogan is the 50. The storms are the second sentence.
- [Peter Ridd, Senate](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Senate/Rural_and_Regional_Affairs_and_Transport/GreatBarrierReef/Report/section?id=committees%2Freportsen%2F024386%2F73345): the 50 is treated as a one-way death. He noted the **2011–2016 recovery** after Yasi that some society papers skipped. AIMS’s own later series made his point without his name on it.
- [AIMS 2022](https://www.aims.gov.au/information-centre/news-and-stories/highest-coral-cover-central-northern-reef-36-years): northern **36%**, central **33%** — regional **highs** in the 36-year record. [AIMS 2026](https://www.aims.gov.au/monitoring-great-barrier-reef/gbr-condition-summary-2025-26): northern **35.1%**, still above the long-term **27%**. A 50% decline to 2012 and a record high a decade later are both in the government ledger. The slogan only kept the first.

Grammar: 50 per cent, 27 years. Logic: a cyclone year is not a tombstone. Rhetoric: declined.
