Harriet Shing, Minister for the Suburban Rail Loop, 21 March 2025. Same day [Infrastructure Australia](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-03-21/victoria-suburban-rail-loop-report-infrastructure-australia/105082444) put a “major risk” sticker on Jacinta Allan’s signature tunnel. Shing’s reply: on time, on budget, trains in 2035.

This card files that slide. It does not file “cancel the loop” and it does not file the opposition’s “fiscal time bomb.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- SRL East (Cheltenham to Box Hill) carries a **$34.5 billion** price from **2020**. IA: “We have **low confidence** in the cost estimate.” Industry-wide escalation since 2020 is a further risk. “Crucial project delivery details related to **actual costs and the funding** of the project were not provided.”
- The same evaluation says the economic case is **likely overstated**, and tells the proponent to write **exit strategies** if the job cannot be delivered. [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/australia-news/2025/mar/21/victorian-government-urged-to-prepare-exit-strategy-for-suburban-rail-loop-as-report-finds-benefits-overstated) ran the same lines the same day.
- “On budget” is a claim about a number. The number is the one the evaluator does not trust.

A budget is a figure you can open. A slogan is a figure you repeat. Open both.
