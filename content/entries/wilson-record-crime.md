29/10/2026. The Opposition Leader lists Victoria's problems on ABC regional radio.

**What the Crime Statistics Agency says** (offences recorded per 100,000 people, years ending June):

- 2025: 9,018.9, the highest in the series back to 2009.
- 2026: 8,679.6, down 3.8 per cent. Second highest.
- 2016: 8,677.1, in an older release. Basically a tie with 2026.

Crime is high. It was a record. It is now a former record.
