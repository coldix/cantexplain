25 January 2021. The TGA provisionally approves Pfizer. [The SMH](https://www.smh.com.au/politics/federal/pfizer-covid-vaccine-provisionally-approved-for-australian-use-20210125-p56wko.html) reports the number that stuck: **95 per cent**. Buried in the same paragraph is the Australian “97”: a **97.5 per cent chance** the efficacy is at least **90.3 per cent**. That is a confidence interval. It is not a second vaccine.

Same page, same day, under “Many unknowns”: **unclear** if it reduces **transmission**. Unclear on **asymptomatic** infection — the trial only tested people who had symptoms. Unclear **how long** it lasts. Scott Morrison: “not a silver bullet.” Greg Hunt: “safe and effective.”

This card files that slide. It does not file “the jab did nothing.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- The 95 was against **symptomatic** COVID in the phase-three cohort, original strain. 170 cases in the interim cut: 162 of them placebo.
- Transmission, silent infection, and duration were listed as unknowns **on approval day**. The slogan that followed — you will not get it, you will not pass it on — is a different sentence.
- The folk “97% effective” is mostly a US line. In Australia the 97.5 sat next to 90.3. Coverage later hit 97 per cent in some age groups. Three different 97s. One slogan.

Grammar: symptomatic, trial strain, two months’ follow-up. Logic: that is not “stops the virus.” Rhetoric: 95, then 97, then immunity.
