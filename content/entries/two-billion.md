Malcolm Turnbull, 16 March 2017. [ABC](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-03-16/snowy-hydro-scheme-funding-boost-to-secure-electricity-supply/8358502): a “**$2 billion** intervention.” Josh Frydenberg, same day: it would “run into the **billions** of dollars.” The precise billions arrived later.

This card files that first number. It does not file “never build pumped hydro.”

**What did have a receipt:**

- Nine months later the [feasibility study](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2017-12-21/snowy-hydro-2.0-could-actually-cost-as-much-as-$4.5b/9277368) printed **$3.8–$4.5 billion**. Paul Broad: “It’s expensive, but it **stacks up** economically.” Also: “It **won’t be a cost to taxpayers**, we will be paying for this out of the balance sheet of Snowy Hydro.” The Commonwealth then bought the whole company.
- August 2023 reset: **$12 billion**. [ANAO, June 2026](https://www.anao.gov.au/work/performance-audit/delivery-of-snowy-two-point-zero): **$11.1 billion** already spent at 31 March 2026, **73 per cent** complete. October 2025, Snowy told the auditor expenditure was “**expected to exceed** the approved project cost of $12 billion.” Extra government support “expected” by the end of FY26. HumeLink, the wire that connects it, is another tab — about **$5 billion**, on NSW consumers.
- [ABC, June 2026](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-03/billions-over-budget-years-late-how-snowy-hydro-2-got-here/106706518): on the $12 billion line, about **$3 million a day** since the announcement. The first number was $2 billion.

Grammar: $2 billion, intervention. Logic: a feasibility band is not a press release. Rhetoric: stacks up, not a cost to taxpayers.
