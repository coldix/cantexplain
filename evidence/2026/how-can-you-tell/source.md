## Verbatim (ABC, 18 February 2026)

Sky News, Monday night, return of wives and children of Islamic State militants:

> I've got no time for radical Islam. Their religion concerns me because of what it says in the Koran. They hate Westerners. That's what it's all about.

> You say, 'Well, there's good Muslims out there.' How can you tell me there are good Muslims?

The ABC headline and dek frame this as “suggesting there are no ‘good’ Muslims.” Same page: two days later she told the ABC she **did not believe** there are no “good” Muslims, and named a non-practising Muslim woman who stood for One Nation.

Qualified apology:

> if she “offended anyone out there that doesn't believe in sharia law, or multiple marriages, or wants to bring ISIS brides in, or people from Gaza that believe in a caliphate”

Then: “In general, that is what they want — a world caliphate. And I am not going to apologise.”

On the same ABC interview she used **18,000 people on ASIO’s watchlist** as the reason “we don’t know who these people are.”
