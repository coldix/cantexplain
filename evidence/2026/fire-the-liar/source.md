## What did have a dated receipt

### $275 household bills

RMIT / ABC Promise Tracker — `abc-275-2023-05-19.html`, `rmit-275.html`  
https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-05-19/promise-check-cut-power-bills-by-275-dollars/101791146  
https://www.rmit.edu.au/about/schools-colleges/media-and-communication/industry/promise-tracker/cut-household-power-bills

Labor’s Powering Australia plan (2022 election): “Independent modelling shows Powering Australia …will bring down household power bills by $275 in 2025.”

Albanese, 3 December 2021 press conference:

> electricity prices fall from the current level by $275 for household[s] by 2025, at the end of our first term, if we are successful.

Asked whether that was possible in the time:

> I don't think, I know. I know because we have done the modelling.

He repeated the $275 line at least until 18 May 2022 (National Press Club). The RepuTex modelling predated Russia’s invasion of Ukraine (24 February 2022). The tracker last updated 19 May 2023; the ABC is no longer updating Promise Tracker.

Household electricity CPI, year to June 2026: **+22.4%**, “largely because government rebates which reduced household electricity bills have ended.”  
https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/cpi-rose-38-year-june-2026  
(Also filed under `evidence/2025/two-meters-one-slogan/abs-cpi-2026-06.html`.)

That is not a $275 cut by 2025.

- negative gearing for residential property limited to new builds

## What the donate list does not cite

Broken word, failed referendum, and a trip over a step share one heading.
