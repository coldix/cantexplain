About One Nation

One Nation was launched by Pauline Hanson on 11th April 1997 in Queensland. Since then we have grown all over the country. 

We are a party that stands for Australia and Australian values. We defend our Constitution and stand up against global agendas, for the individual rights and freedoms of all Australians.

The team at One Nation is your voice in our beautiful country.

Join with us to put people before politics, and together we will continue to fight for Australia and its citizens.
