Downloaded 02/11/2026 (Melbourne) by script.

- Data_Tables_Recorded_Offences_Visualisation_Year_Ending_June_2026.xlsx: CSA, year to June 2026 release (24/09/2026). https://files.crimestatistics.vic.gov.au/2026-09/Data_Tables_Recorded_Offences_Visualisation_Year_Ending_June_2026.xlsx . Table 01 covers years ending June 2017 to 2026.
- CSA_Data_tables_year_ending_June_2018_via_archive.org.xlsx: CSA year to June 2018 release, fetched through archive.org because the CSA site returns 403 to scripts. Original URL: https://www.crimestatistics.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/embridge_cache/emshare/original/public/users/201810/19/37d08fb18/Data_tables_Offence_Visualisation_year_ending_June_2018_V2.xlsx . Gives years ending June 2009 to 2018.
- Data_Tables_LGA_..., Data_Tables_Alleged_Offender_Incidents_..., Data_Tables_Unique_Alleged_Offenders_...: same June 2026 release, kept for the youth figure check.
- csa-recorded-offences-series.csv: totals I computed by summing every offence subgroup in Table 01 (counts and rates). My arithmetic, not a CSA published total. [CHECK] against the CSA headline chart.

Method note: 2009 to 2016 come from the 2018 release; 2017 to 2026 from the 2026 release. CSA revises back years (2017 reads 8,427.0 in the 2018 file and 8,440.3 in the 2026 file), so the 2016 vs 2026 comparison (8,677.1 vs 8,679.6) is too close to call across releases.

Check: (8,679.6 / 9,018.9) minus 1 = minus 3.76%, which rounds to the 3.8% fall ABC reported. So the 3.8% is the offence RATE per 100,000, year to June 2026 vs year to June 2025. Count fell 2.1% (637,984 to 624,624).
