## Verbatim (ABC correction, 14 August 2026)

> ABC Radio South West Victoria: During an interview on 12 August 2026, Victorian Socialists candidate Hera Sheppard stated without contest that Gina Rinehart was “pushing for Australian workers to work for two dollars a day”. This was innaccurate. Rineharts previous comments referred to African workers being willing to work for less than $2 a day, in the context of Australia’s international competitiveness. An on-air correction has been issued.
