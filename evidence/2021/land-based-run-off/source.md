## Verbatim (Reef Authority)

> Land-based run-off remains the greatest contributor to poor water quality in the inshore areas of the Great Barrier Reef and is a major contributor to the current poor state of many inshore marine ecosystems.

> Poor water quality is a major threat to the Great Barrier Reef, particularly…

Pollutants listed: fine sediment, excess nutrients, pesticides — “primarily from agricultural activities.”

Plibersek, 25 July 2024 (on the fishing card’s `plibersek.html`): $200 million for revegetation, grazing management, gully stabilisation — water quality as a UNESCO pillar.
