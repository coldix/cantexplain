## Verbatim (Racism. No Way!)

`racismnoway-white-privilege.html`

> White privilege can be defined as the implicit societal advantages afforded to White people relative to those who experience racism.

> According to Francis Kendall, “White privilege is an institutional (rather than personal) set of benefits granted to those of us who, by race, resemble the people who dominate the powerful positions in our institutions.”

> In order to be more aware of privilege, it is important to think about what it is to see society systemically and structurally instead of only in terms of individuals making individual choices.
