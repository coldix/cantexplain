## Verbatim

> Australians can be reassured that by vaccinating their children against COVID-19 they have done everything possible to keep their child safe from this virus.

> Vaccinating children can also help reduce community transmission and help prevent children passing the virus on to younger siblings, grandparents and the wider community.

Same lines in `news-kids.html` (news.com.au, 10 January 2022 rollout).

## The other meter

Same TGA page already filed: transmission **unclear**; trial did not test asymptomatic; duration unclear. “Everything possible” and “safe from this virus” are a hard stop. A two-dose course was not a force field.

Omicron wave, January 2022, hit as the 5–11 rollout began. School-age infection was not rare among the vaccinated.
