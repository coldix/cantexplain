our vision

There is a deep concern about what the next generation of Australian children are being taught.

From Foundation to Year 10, the Humanities and Social Sciences Curriculum carries undertones of Critical Race Theory, preoccupation with group identities, a reframing of Western Civilisation through the lens of guilt, and a reduced academic rigour, where social sensitivity is given primacy over critical thinking.

The IPA Schools Program calls on all teachers, parents and concerned Australians who value the future of Australia to rebuild education for our children.

We aim to empower teachers and families to take an active role in their children’s education and by doing so, contribute to a flourishing future of Australian society that knows its roots, and can humbly acknowledge its imperfections without erasing its proud achievements.

  • Drawing on our free, practical classroom resources and encouraging others to do so.
