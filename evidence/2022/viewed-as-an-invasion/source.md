## Verbatim (ACARA v9, Year 4 History, AC9HS4K04)

> the effects of contact with other people on First Nations Australians and their Countries/Places following the arrival of the First Fleet and how this was viewed by First Nations Australians as an invasion

SMH, 29 April 2021, on the draft:

> School students across the country will be taught that First Nations Australians experienced European colonisation as an invasion

The wording is in the live v9 scope-and-sequence file.

## The other meter

The Conversation, 22 June 2021: teaching how First Nations Australians “experienced colonisation” is “not necessarily a direct influence of critical race theory.”

Year 9 elaborations (later band) invite discussion of why “invasion”, “colonisation” and “settlement” remain contested. Year 4’s content description does not.

IPA Schools Program (`../2021/not-in-the-curriculum/ipa-vision.html`): HASS “carries undertones of Critical Race Theory.” Locator, not the slogan.

## What this file does not do

It does not file “there was no frontier conflict” or “Mabo was a fiction.” It files “invasion” as Year 4 essential knowledge against the same curriculum’s later concession that the word is contested.
