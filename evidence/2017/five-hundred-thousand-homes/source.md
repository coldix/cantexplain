## The other meter

2,000 MW is **power**. 350 GWh is **energy**. 500,000 homes is a power house-count. “A week” is an energy duration. They are not interchangeable.

2023 reset: capacity revised 10% higher to 2.2 GW; house-count became “three million homes for a week” (see that card). Tank still ~350 GWh.

First power still listed late 2027 as of June 2026. The 2017 lights-on line is still the old scheme.
