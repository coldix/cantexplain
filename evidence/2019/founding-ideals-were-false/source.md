## The other meter

`hnn-harris.html` — Leslie M. Harris, originally Politico, 6 March 2020
https://www.politico.com/news/magazine/2020/03/06/1619-project-new-york-times-mistake-122248

> I listened in stunned silence as Nikole Hannah-Jones… repeated an idea that I had vigorously argued against with her fact-checker: that the patriots fought the American Revolution in large part to preserve slavery in North America.

> I vigorously disputed the claim. Although slavery was certainly an issue in the American Revolution, the protection of slavery was not one of the main reasons the 13 Colonies went to war.

> Far from being fought to preserve slavery, the Revolutionary War became a primary disrupter of slavery in the North American Colonies.

## What this file does not do

It does not file “the 1619 landing did not happen” or “the founders were colour-blind saints.” It files “founding ideals were false” / Revolution-to-protect-slavery against the *Times*’s own fact-checker.
