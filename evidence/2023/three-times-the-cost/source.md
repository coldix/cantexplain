VAGO, 20 March 2024:

> The original Games budget was unrealistically low, but the $6.9 billion cost estimate the government publicly released when it withdrew from the Games was overstated.

> The cost estimate for the Games that the government publicly released in August 2023 of $6.9 billion was overstated and not transparent. It added significant amounts for industrial relations and cost escalation risks. But it did not disclose that the budget already included $1 billion in contingency allowances to cover these and other cost risks.

Fair presentation of the contingency: about **$5.9 billion**, not $6.9 billion.

On the $2.6 billion:

- DJSIR briefed the minister for a high-case gross budget of up to **$3.2 billion**.
- Final submission the same day recommended the low-case **$2.7 billion** (VAGO: change “at the request of the Minister’s office”).
- 2022–23 Budget printed **$2.6 billion**.
- VAGO: using the best-case was “unwarranted optimism bias.” At worst, “unrealistic and misleading.”

Allan, ABC same day as the audit: the report “confirm[s] that we made the right decision.”
